Why integrated IT infrastructure

Isolated systems are no longer the way organizations run.

Modern technology environments are no longer built around isolated systems. Networks, servers, security, surveillance, communication, data and operational systems increasingly need to work together.

Aeon Tech Solutions works as a technology consultant across these layers — assessing what is in place, defining what the requirement actually calls for, and specifying how the parts should work together before anything is procured.

The same engineering habit we apply to industrial plant — understand the installation first, then define the scope — applies here: the architecture is drawn from the operating requirement, not from a product catalogue, and not from whatever a vendor is selling.

Command & control centres

Where every system arrives in one room.

A command centre is the clearest test of integration: video wall, camera feeds, maps, network status, communication and operator positions all have to behave as one environment.

Data centre & computing infrastructure

Racks, power, network, cooling — as one build.

Server and network racks, structured fibre and copper, storage, UPS and power distribution, cooling, environmental monitoring and physical access control are specified together, because a shortfall in any one of them limits the rest.

Tier classification, redundancy levels and uptime commitments are stated only where they are formally designed and certified for the project. [ADD CERTIFICATION IF APPLICABLE]

Security & surveillance

Physical security and cybersecurity, kept distinct and connected.

Cameras, recording and video management protect the site; network security, firewalls and access control protect the system carrying that video. They are different disciplines that have to be designed together.
