Our Position

We support the ESP you already own.

Aeon Tech Solutions is not a general trading house. Our work starts from the equipment: the field arrangement, the electrode profile, the rapping arrangement, the gas conditions and the emission limit the plant has to hold.

That means a request for collecting electrodes is treated as a question about the field it belongs to — and a rising stack reading is treated as a diagnostic problem before it is treated as a purchase order.

We supply mechanical spares, electrical and control equipment, and the engineering services around them: inspection, performance assessment, troubleshooting, optimisation, retrofit and consultancy.

04 — Life Extension

Retrofit & Upgradation

Replacing an ESP is rarely the first answer. In most cases the casing and structure are sound and the limitation sits in the internals, the power supply or the control philosophy.

A retrofit scope defined against measured condition — rather than against a catalogue — extends the performance and useful life of the existing installation at a fraction of replacement cost and downtime.

01Control system retrofitReplacement of obsolete controllers with current-generation control, keeping the existing TR sets where they are sound. 02Power supply upgradeHigh frequency or three-phase power supply in place of conventional single-phase energisation. 03Internals replacementField-by-field replacement of collecting and discharge electrodes with corrected alignment and suspension. 04Rapping system modernisationRapping arrangement and cycle revisions where the dust layer is not being released cleanly.
