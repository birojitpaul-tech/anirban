ESP Discharge Electrodes

Precision-Engineered Discharge Electrodes for Reliable ESP Performance

Discharge electrodes are a critical component of an Electrostatic Precipitator. They operate at a high electrical potential relative to the collecting electrodes and generate the corona discharge required to electrically charge particulate matter in the gas stream. The charged particles then migrate towards the collecting electrodes under the influence of the electrostatic field.

Spiral Discharge Electrodes

Engineered Spiral Profiles for ESP Applications

Spiral electrodes are a specialised discharge-electrode configuration used in certain ESP designs. The continuous helical geometry presents a corona-emitting profile along the whole active length of the electrode, rather than at discrete points.

The exact geometry, pitch, diameter, length and mounting arrangement depend on the ESP design and on the field the electrode is being fitted into.

Spiral discharge electrodes are manufactured to suit the specific electrical and mechanical requirements of the ESP. Geometry and dimensions can be configured according to customer/OEM drawings and application requirements.
