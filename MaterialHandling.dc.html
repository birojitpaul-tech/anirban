Bulk Material Handling & Automatic Bagging Systems

From bulk material feeding to bagging, stitching, palletization and truck loading.

Aeon Tech Solutions provides integrated bulk material handling and automatic bagging solutions for granular and powdered products. Our systems can be engineered to handle the complete material flow — from bulk feeding and conveying through automatic weighing and filling, bag stitching, finished-bag conveying, palletization and truck loading.

Our solutions can be configured for applications such as fertilizers, animal feed, sugar, food products, minerals, chemicals and other free-flowing or suitable powdered and granular materials.

The conveying technology follows the material.

Automatic truck loading systems

The last conveyor ends inside the truck.

Finished bags can be conveyed directly to the truck loading system, reducing manual handling between packaging and dispatch. A telescopic or extendable conveyor advances into the truck body and retracts as loading progresses.

Controlled bag weighing

Weighing set to the bag, not to the machine.

Automatic bagging systems can incorporate weighing controls appropriate to the product and the required bag weight. The control approach is confirmed against the product's flow behaviour and the target weight rather than quoted as a fixed figure.

Dust control

Dust control for powdered materials.

Powdered materials may require appropriate dust-control measures at the points where product is transferred or released. Provision is decided from the product and the plant environment; no emission level is claimed here.
