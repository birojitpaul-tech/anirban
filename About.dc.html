An engineering company, structured around equipment.

Aeon Tech Solutions supplies specialised industrial equipment, spares and technical support across three verticals — air pollution control, cable management and bulk material handling — to heavy process industry in India and internationally.

Who We Are

We start from the equipment, not the catalogue.

Most industrial equipment in service today was installed years or decades ago, under specifications that have since changed and by suppliers who may no longer support it. The plants running that equipment need two things at once: parts that fit, and someone who understands why the equipment is behaving the way it is.

Aeon Tech Solutions was set up to do both. We supply spares and equipment, and we provide the inspection, troubleshooting, optimisation and retrofit engineering that sits around them. Our focus areas are deliberately narrow — electrostatic precipitators and air pollution control, wire mesh cable management systems, and bulk material handling — because depth in a small number of equipment types is what makes the technical judgement useful.

We work directly with plant engineering and maintenance teams, and alongside established international technology providers where a proven machine platform is the right answer.

How We Work

Four steps between an enquiry and a working solution.

01

Understand the equipment

Drawings, nameplate data, old datasheets, photographs or simply the symptom on the plant. We work from whatever record exists.

02

Define the real scope

Assessment of what is actually limiting performance, so the scope covers the cause rather than the most visible component.

03

Supply and execute

Supply to the installed profile, with site supervision through replacement, retrofit or commissioning.

04

Stay available afterwards

Spare standardisation, follow-up assessment and support at the next shutdown, so the record does not have to be rebuilt each time.
