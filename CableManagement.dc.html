Overview

Wire mesh trays, accessories, supports and underground protection.

At Aeon Tech Solutions, cable management is one of our core focus areas. We specialise in wire mesh (basket) cable trays — a modern alternative to conventional perforated and ladder-type trays — along with the accessories, supports, and underground cable protection systems needed to complete a full cable routing solution.

Our systems are used across data centres, power and process plants, commercial buildings, railways, and infrastructure projects, wherever reliable, low-maintenance cable containment is critical.

The problem

Cables will find their own route unless you give them one.

Every added circuit that goes in without containment costs something later: nobody can trace a fault, nothing can be added without disturbing what is already there, heat has nowhere to go, and the whole bundle becomes a fire and rodent risk.

Containment is cheap at the design stage and expensive to retrofit. This is the work we are here to do.

Why Wire Mesh Cable Trays

Conventional trays create the problems. Mesh solves them by design.

Conventional cable trays (perforated sheet and ladder type) require heavy support structures, on-site welding and cutting, and are prone to sharp edges that damage cables and injure installers.

10×

Up to 10x faster installation — no welding, cutting, or grinding required on site.

70–80%

Lighter than conventional trays, reducing the load on support structures and cable racks.

3

Only 3 accessories (connector, screw, and connecting tab) are needed to build almost any configuration — straight runs, bends, risers, and branches.

SAFETY

No sharp edges — safer for cables and for installation crews.

THERMAL

Excellent ventilation — open mesh design keeps cables cool and allows easy inspection and cleaning.

ROUTING

Free-form routing — the mesh can be shaped on site with a simple cutting tool, so no pre-engineered bends, tees, or elbows are needed.

RELIABILITY

No hidden spaces for pests or rodents, unlike enclosed trays — reducing the risk of short circuits and fire caused by rodent damage.

500 kg

Per welded cross junction on the grades we supply — a light structure that still carries a full cable load between supports.

LOGISTICS

Trays nest into each other, so a full site quantity ships and stores in a fraction of the space a sheet tray needs.

PROJECT COST

Significant cost savings across labour, logistics, storage, and accessories compared to conventional tray systems.

Built to Handle Corrosion

Pay for the protection level your site actually needs.

Cable trays are exposed to a wide range of environments — indoor, outdoor, marine, chemical, and offshore — each with different corrosion risks.

We help customers select the right grade based on site conditions, so trays deliver a long service life without over-specifying cost.
