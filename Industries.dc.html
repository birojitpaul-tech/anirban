Industries We Serve

Heavy process industry, where gas cleaning, cable routing and bulk material flow are all operational constraints rather than line items. Each sector below lists the equipment we typically work on and the failure modes we are usually called about.

01

Cement & Lime

Kiln, raw mill, cooler and coal mill ESPs operating on abrasive, high-temperature dust with wide swings in gas volume and moisture. Most installations are decades old and are now held to emission limits set long after commissioning.

Typical Applications

Kiln & raw mill ESP Clinker cooler ESP Coal mill ESP Cement packing plant handling

What We Are Called About

Rising stack emissions after a process change Distorted or eroded collecting electrodes Rapping shaft and hammer failures Obsolete controllers with no spare backing
02

Steel & Iron

Sinter plant, blast furnace, sponge iron and rolling mill gas cleaning duties, often with sticky or high-resistivity dust and aggressive thermal cycling. Cable routing in these plants also has to survive heat, vibration and mechanical damage.

Typical Applications

Sinter plant ESP Blast furnace gas cleaning Sponge iron / DRI kiln ESP Rolling mill fume extraction

What We Are Called About

Fields tripping on low secondary voltage Broken discharge electrodes and frames Insulator flashover and tracking Cable tray corrosion and heat damage
03

Power Generation

Coal-fired utility and captive boiler ESPs, where a single field out of service is measurable at the stack and a forced outage carries a generation penalty. High frequency and three-phase power supply retrofits are frequently the highest-return intervention here.

Typical Applications

Utility boiler ESP Captive power plant ESP Waste-to-energy gas cleaning Ash handling and cable routing

What We Are Called About

Emission limit compliance after a coal change TR set failures and long lead times Control system obsolescence Shutdown spares planning
04

Non-Ferrous Metals

Smelter, roaster and refinery gas cleaning where the dust carries metal values and the gas stream is corrosive. Recovery of collected material matters as much as the emission limit.

Smelter and roaster ESP Acid plant gas cleaning Corrosion-grade cable tray systems
05

Mining & Minerals

Mineral drying, calcination and processing plants, where abrasive material and continuous duty drive both gas cleaning and bulk handling requirements.

Dryer and calciner ESP Conveying and storage systems Bagging and truck loading
06

Chemical & Process

Corrosive and abrasive duties across chemical, carbon black and fertiliser production — including valve bag filling of fine powders and stainless or non-metallic cable containment.

Process gas cleaning Valve bag filling lines SS316 and PVC tray systems
07

Other Process Industries

Fertiliser, sugar, paper, food grain and allied plants, plus data centres and infrastructure projects served through our cable management vertical.

Fertiliser and sugar plants Rice mills and grain handling Data centres and infrastructure
